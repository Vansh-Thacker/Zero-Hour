# The Endurance Race

The business is the car. Each control fits a part of the car or the support team. Each phase of the attack is one stint (one driver's spell at the wheel). Most laps wins.

**The least that counts as this theme:** a 2D track where progress changes with the player's choices, a finish result, and an explanation of how choices map to the race.

## What is here

`reference.json` gives a car part and a one-line meaning for each of the 18 controls and the three controls already in place. For example, email security is the front grille: it catches debris before it reaches the engine.

There is no supplied formula for laps or speed. You design how control scores and question answers turn into race performance, and you explain it to the player.

## Artwork you could use

- `2-extend/artwork/race/`: 2D race pieces
- `2-extend/artwork/3d/cars/`: eight team cars
- `2-extend/artwork/3d/track/`: track pieces, including pylon bridges, tunnels and gates
- `2-extend/artwork/3d/race_parts/`: a model for each car part
- `2-extend/artwork/scenery/` and `2-extend/artwork/3d/scenery/`: turbines, pylons, trees and buildings
- `2-extend/worlds/`: a ready-assembled race circuit

## Keep in mind

- Show each control's real name with its car part.
- Driving physics and 3D are optional. A simple 2D track is enough.
- The race must not suggest that detecting an attack always prevents the damage.
- Use the same four-round rules as the core game (see `BRIEF.html` in the pack folder).
